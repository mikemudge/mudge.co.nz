# Wildergrove Roadmap

## Milestone 1 — complete

The full loop is playable start to finish: talk to Elder Rin, gather wood,
build a campfire, branch into farming/mining/cooking/crafting, build a forge,
upgrade tools, enter the mine, and work through the three Elder's Request
bounties (pumpkin soup, iron hoard, pumpkin harvest). All six required
pillars are in - mining, farming, cooking, crafting, building, and water
(rivers/streams/ponds, a limited watering can, wells) - with a full save/load
loop and an autosave.

## Ideas for later (not scoped or started)

- **Animal husbandry** - chickens (eggs), cows (milk), feed production and a
  coop/barn building to house them.
- **Food spoilage/preservation** - cooked dishes and raw crops degrade over
  time; jars/preserves as a crafting line to stop that.
- **Recipe/blueprint discovery** - stop handing every recipe out for free;
  find blueprints in world notes/ruins, or unlock via trial-and-error at a
  station.
- **Multiple mine levels** - a second (and later) floor below the current
  one, presumably tougher ore/hazards, reached via a new descend point.
- **Deeper crafting chains + wearables** - multi-step production (e.g. cloth
  from plant fiber, leather from... something), plus equippable gear beyond
  the four tools.
- **Building interiors** - walls, doors, and an actual indoor/outdoor
  distinction, rather than every building being a prop sitting on the open
  grid.
- **Fuller exploration layer** - more of the biome/path/ruins texture the
  map has room for, beyond the current four regions.
- **More endgame content** - a fresh quest batch once a player's cleared the
  current chain, now that there's a full pillar set to draw objectives from.

None of these are committed to - just the backlog from ideas that came up
along the way, kept here so they don't get lost between sessions.
